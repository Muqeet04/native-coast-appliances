The craft

From a galley refit to a full open-plan kitchen with island and larder — made and fitted by one firm.

Bespoke cabinetry

Hand-built shaker and flat-panel fronts, sprayed any colour you can name, scribed to walls that aren't quite straight.

Stone worktops

Marble, quartzite and granite — templated by laser, book-matched by eye, folded over island ends in a waterfall.

Brass, taps & lighting

Unlacquered brass that ages where you touch it, and hand-blown pendants hung to the millimetre over the island.

Full renovation & fit

Strip-out, first fix, plaster and floors — one crew, one schedule, and a kitchen that lands in five working days.
