A kitchen is furniture — built once, built properly.
We make one kitchen at a time. Cabinets built and sprayed at our own bench, stone templated by laser and matched by eye, and a fitting crew that has been with us for years. No flat-pack carcasses, no subcontractors — just joinery, stone and brass, done properly.
